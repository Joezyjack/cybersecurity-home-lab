EXERCISE 02: LAN DISCOVERY

Tool: Netdiscover
Network: 192.168.100.0/24

OBJECTIVE:
Identify active devices on the local network using ARP discovery.

RESULTS:

1. IP: 192.168.100.1
   MAC: 00:90:4a:31:94:b0
   Vendor: Concur System Technologies
   Device: Default Gateway/Router

2. IP: 192.168.100.41
   MAC: ea:09:29:bf:9a:85
   Vendor: Unknown
   Device: iPhone

SUMMARY:
Netdiscover captured 3 ARP request/reply packets
and identified 2 unique hosts.

COMPARISON:
Nmap discovered 3 hosts, including Kali Linux.
Netdiscover identified 2 other devices.

CONCLUSION:
Both tools successfully identified the router
and iPhone on the local network.
